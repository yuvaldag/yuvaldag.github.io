Bio

I am a Senior Lecturer (equivalent to Assistant Professor) at the School of Computer Science, Tel Aviv University. My research is in Machine Learning Theory, with connections to probability theory, game theory, statistics, complexity, privacy and applied machine learning.

Education

  • Ph.D. in Computer Science, Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), EECS, 2018–2023. Advisor: Prof. Constantinos Daskalakis.
    Recipient of the Facebook Research Fellowship (Machine Learning), 2021–2022.
    Thesis received the George M. Sprowls PhD Thesis Award in Artificial Intelligence and Decision Making.
  • M.Sc. in Computer Science, Technion – Israel Institute of Technology, 2015–2017. Advisor: Prof. Yuval Filmus.
  • B.Sc. in Computer Science, Technion – Israel Institute of Technology, 2011–2015.

Postdoctoral studies

  • Postdoctoral Researcher, UC Berkeley, 2023–2024. Studied learning theory under the Machine Learning Pod of the Simons Institute for the Theory of Computing and the Foundations of Data Science Institute (FODSI).
