Start with the basics. The language decides which of your setups the song is filed under, and the name is what you'll see in your list.

Now get timed lyrics — lyrics with a time on every line, so the words follow the music. Two ways; either is fine.

Look for them online

  1. Tap the button — it searches Google for your song on lyric sites.
  2. Open a result and look for lyrics starting with times like [00:17.54].
  3. Select them all, copy, and come back.
or

Have ChatGPT listen to it

  1. Tap Ask ChatGPT. In the app the song file and the request go over together, so there is nothing to attach yourself.
  2. Send it, and give it a minute — it is listening to the whole song.
  3. It answers with a box of [00:17.54] … lines. Tap Copy on that box and come back.

No timed lyrics anywhere? Plain lyrics are fine too — paste them one line each in the next step. The song just won't scroll by itself.

Now get the translation from ChatGPT. It writes the translation of every line plus the meaning of each individual word, which is what lets you tap a word later.

  1. Tap Open ChatGPT — the request is copied for you.
  2. In ChatGPT: paste, and send.
  3. When it finishes, copy its whole answer.
  4. Come back and paste it in the next step.

Paste ChatGPT's whole answer here. Don't tidy it up — the layout is what the app reads.
