Your songs

Start with the basics. The language decides which of your setups the song is filed under, and the name is what you'll see in your list.

Now find timed lyrics. These are lyrics with a time on every line, so the words can follow the music. The file type is called LRC.

  1. Tap the button below — it searches Google for your song on lyric sites.
  2. Open a result and look for lyrics that start with times like [00:17.54].
  3. Select them all and copy.
  4. Come back here and continue to the next step.

No timed lyrics anywhere? You can still paste plain lyrics in the next step — one line each. The song just won't scroll by itself.

Paste the lyrics you copied. Timed lines look like [00:17.54] first line…. Plain lines (no times) are fine too.

Now get the translation from ChatGPT. It writes the translation of every line plus the meaning of each individual word, which is what lets you tap a word later.

  1. Tap Open ChatGPT — the request is copied for you.
  2. In ChatGPT: paste, and send.
  3. When it finishes, copy its whole answer.
  4. Come back and paste it in the next step.

Paste ChatGPT's whole answer here. Don't tidy it up — the layout is what the app reads.
